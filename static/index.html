What does this cron do?

Paste an expression. You get a plain reading and the next run times. The form works with JavaScript off.

Times stay in the zone you pick. This server does not guess your phone zone.

How

1

Five fields, in order: minute, hour, day of month, month, weekday.

2

Next times come from croniter, in the zone you picked. Daylight saving is that zone's problem, and we follow it.

3

If day of month and weekday are both set, standard cron fires when either matches. The page says so.

Limits

  • Five fields, or @hourly, @daily, @weekly, @monthly, @yearly. @midnight and @annually too.
  • No seconds field. No @reboot. That one is a boot hook, not a clock time.
  • This is crontab cron. systemd OnCalendar is a different language.
  • 8 next runs. 60 explains per IP per hour.
  • Names like MON and JAN are fine. Quartz extras (L, W, #) are not.
